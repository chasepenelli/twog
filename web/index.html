01 — WHAT'S NEW

New primitives for agentic science.

Agents are good at proposing. The hard part is making what they produce trustworthy, portable, and cumulative. These are the building blocks that do that.

01

Proof Capsule

A portable, verifiable unit of computational evidence — who ran it, against what, the directional signal, and its limitations. Evidence you can review, move, and trust on its own.

02

Expert-gated Lane

Pluggable compute — docking, co-folding, MD, omics — that any collaborator or agent can run. The science is modular; new methods slot in without touching the core.

03

The Write Gate

Operator approval is the only path evidence promotes onto a claim. Agents propose; humans dispose. Trust by construction, not by hope.

04

Checkpoint & Handoff

Long GPU work pauses at 40% and someone else checks it out and resumes — proven on real hardware. Compute as a leasable, resumable resource.

05

Falsification-first Crux

Cheap, pre-registered tests that try to kill the hypothesis first. We falsified our own flagship idea and caught a tumor-purity confound before it cost a thing.

06

The Flywheel — (3,3)

Every run gives back: open datasets, signature registries, models. Each contribution lowers the cost of the next. Positive-sum by design.
