01 · reference study

What to steal from The Nifty Portal

The Awwwards SOTD reference isn’t a literal 3-D wormhole — it’s a type-as-art, one-page, kinetic experience built with WebGL + Webpack + GSAP. Its power is restraint plus motion: oversized retro typography, marquee bands that scroll sideways while you scroll down, and a single continuous narrative instead of stacked “sections.” That maps cleanly onto our scientific-brutalist white/black direction.

Signature 1

Kinetic marquee type

Oversized words scroll horizontally as the page scrolls vertically — type is the artwork. Cheap to build (GSAP), huge presence, zero imagery required.

Signature 2

One continuous scene

No visible section seams. Acts blend via pinning, scrub and reveal — the page feels authored, not assembled. Justifies having only a few “sections.”

Signature 3

WebGL as texture

WebGL used as an ambient field behind type, not a literal object — exactly our hero contour-field shader. Atmosphere, not spectacle.

Signature 4

Editorial restraint

Tight palette, enormous negative space, one idea per screen. Trust is carried by typographic confidence, not decoration.

Sources: awwwards.com/inspiration/the-nifty-portal-nft-art-project · awwwards.com/sites/the-nifty-portal · tech: WebGL · Webpack · GSAP.
